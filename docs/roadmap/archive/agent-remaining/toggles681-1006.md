Keyboard: Enter on every trigger above toggles (the sweep's `k1`/`k2`), Escape

button's Enter picks the tool by design; ArrowDown/Escape are its keys.

that open on hover and on click, and already close on a later click).

## The fix

One shared change: the outside-press closer (settings-wiring.js, `pointerdown`,
capture) leaves a press on `[aria-haspopup][aria-expanded='true']` to the
opener's own click, which toggles. The chip menu names the chip as its opener
(`menu._escapedOpener`, `aria-expanded`) so it joins that rule. The Shapes
button's gestures toggle in whiteboard.js.
