## CI

The E2E job runs every spec (`npx playwright test`, 11 files, 47 tests), not
only smoke.spec.js: three workers (files side by side, tests in a file in
order), one retry on CI, a 30 s navigation timeout, `reloadApp` for every
"after a reload" (a reload then a second goto hung now and then). Measured
here with CI=1 and no retries, under load 6 to 8 on four cores: 47 passed in
4.1 min (two flakes in the run before, both fixed at the cause: 24107d3 and
the held autosave in documents.spec.js). No fixed sleep waits for state; the
two left are soaks (smoke's console-error window, the "stays open" check in
documents.spec.js). The smoke-only job
took 1 min 50 s end to end on CI, so the whole job should land near 7 min of
its 15. Not yet run on CI (this branch is not pushed).
