# atlas669-1006: remaining

INBOX 669 built (a click in the large view tilts and eases back; the arms move at most 3.3 degrees a frame). Measured with `scratchpad/ui-sweeps/atlas669-clicks.js` (20 real clicks per case; `acts-<pose>-<look>` cases play every arm act and poke mood), before against after on two servers.

Open:
- Whole-body moves are as fast as drawn: a hop, a cheer, a startle (`nmb-hop`, 14px up in about 240ms) move the head 14 to 17px a frame in the large view (2.2 times). They are the body's own designed velocity, not a snap, and no click in the large view plays one (a poke there is a wiggle or a wave). Decision needed if the large view should slow them: recommendation, a gentler `nmb-hop` only under `.nm-viewer-figure`.
- A click in Atlas's own large view (not the companion visiting) costs 22 to 44ms of `setAtlasMood` (`nameMarkBuddyBlend` over every layer's animations, then `atlasApply` on each of 12 layers); on a loaded machine its frame runs 50 to 100ms. Not a jump, a stall. `scratchpad/ui-sweeps/atlas669-clickcost.js`.
- The generic (non-Atlas) faces' arms are driven by the stylesheet directly, with no rig and so no speed cap; their wave and hide keyframes now start and end at rest but their fastest segment was not measured.
- Chromium quirk, seen once (`atlas669-blendcheck.js`): a blend's implicit end keyframe held `rotate` at its start value while a CSS animation that starts at the same value ran under it. The blend now eases only values that moved, which avoids it; not reduced to a minimal case (`atlas669-implicit.js` with a plain CSS animation behaves).
- Not verified: the owner's desktop window at its own refresh rate; every number here is headless Chromium on a machine at load 3 to 7, frames scaled to 60 a second by their timestamps.
