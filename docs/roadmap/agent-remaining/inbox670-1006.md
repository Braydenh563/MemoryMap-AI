# inbox670-1006: a template on a rootless map, and the last wells of four

INBOX 670. Worktree `agent-a3468078f75b84fe4`, merged from
`claude/mini-release-0.4.1`.

## Result

- A map template placed on a map with no central topic: several top-level
  topics go under a new central topic named after the template, one top-level
  topic is the centre, a map with a centre is unchanged (server
  `_place_branch`, `_map_has_no_centre`; also a new map started from a map
  template). `tests/test_board_library.py` (five tests).
- `dropplace.js` (map half, free layout, zoom 0.5, 1, 2): drag and click 0 to
  0.2px with and without a root, no topics over each other, one Ctrl+Z each.
- Font and Density (Settings, Appearance), the Graph's Layout and the
  suggestions inbox's kinds are selects; every id and handler kept
  (`#font-seg`, `#density-seg`, `#graph-layout`; the inbox's id is
  `#inbox-kind`, the reveal targets follow). Font rows are set in their own
  face (`data-menu-class` on an option, `sheets-selects.js`).
- `#note-picker-sources` stays a `.seg` of five: DESIGN.md's picker rows pin
  it as equal segments, each with a count. `SEG_OF_FOUR_PLACED` ends with that
  one entry and the reason.
- `seg670.js` (settings, graph, inbox) passes at 390 and 1440, light and dark;
  `kg9inbox.js` at 1440 light and 390 dark; `seg665.js` 22 of 22.
