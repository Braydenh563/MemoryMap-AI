# arch1005: what the architecture fix agent left open (2026-10-05)

The audit is `scratchpad/audit1005/arch.md`; each finding fixed carries
`FIXED <commit>` on its line. Open, most severe first:

  measured with 384-wide vectors at load 10 to 13 a 400-word save was 390 to
  615 ms at 5,000 notes against 61 to 96 ms at 500 (4,300 ms before): the
  remaining growth is numpy over the whole matrix (the kNN product, the
  matrix's own `vstack` on each new vector) and the lexical candidates on a
  small vocabulary. Not met: the brief's "within 20% of 500". A `PUT` that
  changes the text still embeds on the request (the brief's step 5).

  turn (`core/model_gate.py`); `file-entry` still shares the one-wide model
  lane with captions, kept on purpose (two models loaded at once is the
  memory failure the lane exists to prevent).
