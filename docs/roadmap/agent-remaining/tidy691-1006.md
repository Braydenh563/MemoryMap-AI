## Step 1: what existed (audit, 2026-10-06)

Measured on the showcase notebook (`scratchpad/ui-sweeps/seed-showcase.py`,
77 notes, 87 links, 61 tags) with no chat model, the sandbox's embedder:

| Thing | Count |
| --- | --- |
| Links with the generic "similar in meaning" | 48 of 87 (55%), all deduced (`reason_confidence` 0.57 to 0.85) |
| Links with a reason in words (the seed's, a person's) | 39 |
| Tags used once | 37 of 61 |
| Notes with open tag suggestions (offered at filing, not taken) | 20 |
| Notes the filer was sure of (`ai_confidence` 100) | 75; 2 at 0 |

- `manager.create_link` -> `_deduce_reason` wrote the literal "similar in
  meaning" for every link made without words (the wiki `[[name]]` links
  too). `ai/relations.recognise` (KG2) already named shared entities,
  neighbours and rare tags, but only for suggestions. The reasons backfill
  (`/entries/links/backfill-reasons`) wrote more "similar in meaning" without
  a model; its one button was inside the suggestions inbox's Links pane.
- Tags are the person's: filing never applies one, it keeps up to five
  `suggested_tags`. Tags another actor wrote come from the agent's tag tools
  (`ai:<tool>`), the autonomous `stale` tag and passive capture; the audit
  log's `actor` is the only provenance. There is no per-tag confidence.
- Management that existed: the tag manager (rename, merge, remove, Used
  once, look-alike Merge) behind the Notes ⋯ menu, the sidebar's Tags row,
  chips, Settings and the palette; Manage categories; the Select bar's bulk
  tags, move, favourite, archive, space; `is:review` typed into the filter
  or a dashboard widget; Find duplicates in Settings, Data only; Explain
  your existing links inside the suggestions inbox. Nothing for weak links,
  tags another actor added, short notes or stale reminders.

## What landed

- **Specific link reasons** (`entry/link_wording.py`, pure;
  `entry/link_facts.py`, the facts and counts, kept per notebook state). In
  order: one note names the other, shared tags (rarest first, a tag on more
  than a quarter of the notebook says nothing), shared names (entities, or a
  capital mid-sentence), shared rare words, same category within a week. Two
  clues at most, under 80 characters; "similar in meaning" only when nothing
  else is shared. Used by `_deduce_reason` (every new link), the link
  suggestion preview, and Tidy. Measured on the showcase notebook: before 48
  of 87 links generic; Tidy's review offered 38; after the background pass,
  10 of 89 generic (the two new links made by the 709 sweep got specific
  reasons at creation).
- **Tidy** (`entry/tidy.py`, `api/routes_tidy.py`, `frontend/js/tidy.js`,
  `frontend/css/tidy-lazy.css`): nine reviews, each a rule, a list with a
  why and a preview per row, apply on the ticked rows (recomputed on the
  server), one `tidied` log row and one Undo (toast, Ctrl+Z, Recent runs).
  Showcase counts after `tidy691-seed.py`: links to explain 38, weak links
  4, tags Atlas added 1, tags used once 41, look-alike tags 1, uncategorised
  2, duplicates 1, short notes 1, stale reminders 1.
- **Automation**: Apply automatically per review (`tidy_auto`, off by
  default), run after each filing as `system:tidy`, throttled to once per 30
  s; never for duplicates, short notes or tags used once. The link reason
  pass is a durable job (`tidy-link-reasons`, batch lane), stoppable from
  Background tasks between chunks.
- **INBOX 709**: a suggested link is one interactive chip (≈, the note, a +
  at its end), named "Link to <first line>"; the separate + button is gone.

## Found, not fixed

- The Notes dock wraps to two rows from about 1280 to 1500 when the
  connections rail is open, measured with Tidy hidden (90px); Tidy's 40px
  extends that band to about 1640 (1600: 50px without it, 90 with). Moving
  the words-hidden threshold from 1400 to 1680 fixed both, but the
  breakpoint lint (tests/test_breakpoints.py) allows no new width query, so
  the dock needs its own rethink (fewer worded controls above 1400, or the
  rail narrower), not a query.
- The card tooltip and the graph still show a deduced reason as "(73%
  confidence, deduced)", not the strength word; the boot scripts are at
  their gzip cap. Tidy's rows show it ("some likeness (73%)").
- `/entries/{id}/related` lists notes that are already linked, so a press on
  one answers 400 "already linked" (pre-existing; seen in the 709 sweep).
- `tests/test_feature_catalog.py` failed on the integration branch before
  this work (`openNewMeeting`, meetings.js missing from its scan list); the
  merge carries the other agent's fix, and tidy.js is added beside it.
