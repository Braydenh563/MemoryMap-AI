# clip685-1006: focus rings and edges cut off by clipping containers

INBOX 685 (the owner, with a screenshot of the Dashboard constellation's
Regenerate ring cut along its left edge: "a lot of borders get cut off on an
edge"), with 694's cut pills and two text reports ("Categories" and "Skill
logs", the "g" cut) and the New meeting sheet. Built; this file holds what is
left.

## What exists

`scratchpad/ui-sweeps/clip685.js` (`clip685-all.sh` runs 1440 and 390, light
and dark): every visible focusable control, focused by keyboard, its ring's outer
box against every clipping ancestor (overflow, `contain: paint`, clip-path;
absolute and fixed boxes only against their containing block's clippers); the
borders and the chosen segment of a plain edge against the same clippers; every
single line of text against the same (the descender pass). Fixes are in
08-consistency.css "Ring room", held by `tests/test_ring_room.py`;
DESIGN.md names the rule under "One focus ring".

## Not swept yet (add to the sweep, then fix at the container)

- An open board or map, the graph's node popup and controls sheet at 390,
  the print dialog, the lightbox, the confirm dialog, the onboarding tour, the
  Attach dialog, the date and time pickers, every kebab menu on a card.
- Another look than Classic (`LOOK=`), `CONTRAST=on`, forced colours, and
  Large text with Spacious density: a ring's room is fixed (4px), but a
  container's own padding is not.
- Hover and active states that draw an edge (the sweep reads the ring and the
  resting edge only).
- `scroll-padding`: a control focused by Tab inside a scroller is scrolled to
  the scrollport's edge, ring half cut, until the next Tab. The sweep exempts a
  control at a scrolling edge on purpose (that is scroll position, not the
  container). A `scroll-padding: var(--ring-room)` on the page scrollers
  (`.tab-page`, `.tab-main`, `.modal-content`, `.library-view-section`) would
  close it; not measured, because the sweep does not tab.

## Found, not fixed

- The sweep skips a control that sits at the end of a scroller with more
  content past it, and then every outer clipper on that side; a real cut that
  coincides with a scrolled edge is not seen.
- A ring on a control in a body-level fixed sheet that touches the viewport
  edge (the phone Writing room's draft textarea at the bottom) is treated as
  scroll position; it is not a measured fix.
- Text that is cut by a fixed `height` without `overflow` (a child painted
  over the box) is not seen; only clipped single lines are.

## Not verified

- A real desktop window (WebView2) at 125 or 150 percent scale: fractional
  pixels can put a hairline border 0.4px off an edge; the sweep's tolerance is
  0.4px.
- The 694 pills were measured as box geometry (30px segments in a 30px inside)
  and by the sweep; the owner's two screenshots were not reproduced pixel for
  pixel.
