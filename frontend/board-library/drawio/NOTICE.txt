Shapes in this folder are converted from the stencil libraries of draw.io
(jgraph/drawio, https://github.com/jgraph/drawio): basic, flowchart, arrows,
bpmn and networks. Copyright JGraph Ltd. Licensed under the Apache License,
Version 2.0 (the LICENSE file in this folder).

Changes: the stencil XML drawing operations were converted to SVG path data
(move, line and cubic curves only) with scratchpad/stencils/convert_stencils.py,
and each stencil's connection constraints were kept as ports. Names, geometry
and constraints are otherwise the upstream's.

Upstream asks that its icon sets and stencil libraries, and derivatives of
them, are not used in or incorporated into Atlassian products or products
distributed through the Atlassian marketplace or plugin ecosystem, without
written permission (src/main/webapp/stencils/LICENSE upstream). MemoryMap AI
is neither. Anyone redistributing these files must keep this notice.
